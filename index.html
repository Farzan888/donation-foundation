Our story

Kindness, organised well.

Mukta Helping Hands Foundation was founded on a simple belief: every act of kindness matters. We are not building another charity register — we're building a movement, one that carries forward a legacy of compassion into visible, measurable action.

Our vision is a world where kindness reaches everyone. We get there by choosing depth over breadth: one cause done visibly and honestly well, before we take on the next.

Compassionate Transparent Trustworthy Hopeful Community-driven Action-oriented

We believe every act of kindness matters. Through collective compassion and purposeful action, we aim to create opportunities, support communities, and change lives.

The person behind Mukta

Tanya Choudhury

Founder & Chairperson

Mukta Helping Hands Foundation carries forward a legacy of compassion — Mukta's own — into something people can see, join and measure. Tanya started this not to build "another NGO," but a movement: one where every act of kindness, however small, is treated as the point of the whole exercise.

Kindness has the power to transform lives. My role is simply to make sure that power finds somewhere honest to go.

She leads the foundation's partnerships, its board, and its public story — while making sure the flagship programme is chosen with care, not haste, before a single rupee is spent on promotion.
